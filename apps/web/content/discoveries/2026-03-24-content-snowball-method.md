# Content Snowball: 30 Days of Content From One Topic in 40 Minutes

You sit down to create content. And right there, at the start, is that familiar void. What angle? What's new? What's worth saying?

The better question: what if you didn't have to answer it every time?

Content Snowball takes one topic and pulls it through three layers: seven angles, twenty-one subtopics, sixty-three individual pieces across platforms. One think produces a month's output.

The skill runs in forty minutes. Topic in, content calendar out.

## Why It Matters for Applied Leverage

Our content machine runs on this. The offer pipeline needs volume. More posts, more reach, more leads. But without a system, you're just trading time for output and burning out.

This is the system. One deep session, then you assemble, not invent. It also feeds transcript-atomizer for video work, and the output becomes expert-panel-gate input. The whole thing keeps turning without you staring at a blank page.

## The Catch

The forty-minute runtime assumes your topic is well-defined. "I want to write about AI" doesn't work. You need a specific problem or insight.

And sixty-three pieces is a lot to actually publish. The system gives you raw material. You still need to show up. The snowball gives you bullets, not the gun.
