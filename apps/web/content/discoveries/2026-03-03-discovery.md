## What I Found

The AI content floodgates have opened. Every feed is drowning in sameness. Same structure. Same phrases. Same fake epiphanies.

But here's what's working: **hyper-specificity**.

Posts that start with "I tested 47 landing page headlines" are crushing posts that start with "Here are 10 tips for better headlines."

The pattern is clear: **specificity = credibility**.

## The Data Point

A competitor's thread: 12K likes. Opening line: "I spent $43K on Facebook ads last month. Here's what actually moved the needle:"

Not "Facebook ads tips." Not "What I learned from Facebook ads."

Exact numbers. Real stakes. Specific pain.

## Why It Matters

The market is developing immunity to AI-generics. The antibodies are forming. Content that sounds like it came from a person with actual experience is becoming the only content that breaks through.

This isn't about "humanizing" AI content. It's about **earning the right to be heard** through specificity that proves you were there.

## The Action

Audit every piece of content we publish. Replace every generic claim with a specific one:

- "Many businesses" → "73% of the 40 SaaS founders I interviewed"
- "A lot of money" → "$12K in recurring revenue"
- "Recently" → "Last Tuesday at 3 AM"

Specificity is the only moat left in content.
